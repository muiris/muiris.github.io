FAQs

What's the dress code?

Smart casual to formal. August in Dublin is mild—consider bringing a light layer.

Is parking available?

Limited on-site. Use nearby car parks: St Stephen's Green Shopping Centre or Drury Street. Street parking available.

Can I bring a plus one?

If invited with a guest, let us know their name in the RSVP. Unsure? Contact Christina.

Dietary requirements?

Please let us know in the RSVP form. We'll ensure suitable options are available.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 2:00 PM. Private entrance at No. 85 St Stephen's Green will be clearly marked.

The venue has accessible entrances and facilities. Contact Christina for specific needs.
